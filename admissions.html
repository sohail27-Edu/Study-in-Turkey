Admission Process

Follow these simple steps to apply to universities in Türkiye.

Step-by-Step Guide

Step 1

Choose a university and your desired program.

Step 2

Prepare your documents (passport, educational certificates, transcript, photograph, etc.).

Step 3

Submit your online application through the university portal.

Step 4

Receive your admission or acceptance letter.

Step 5

Apply for a Turkish student visa with the required documents.

Step 6

Travel to Türkiye and complete your university registration.
